## Different-region context

Label criteria named **Previous Region** and **Next Region** use the previous and next *different* region visited, stored as `last_region_visited` and `next_region_visited`. Consecutive fixations within the same region do not change these values.

For immediate fixation adjacency, inspect `previous_fixation_region` and `next_fixation_region`. These fields are informative but are not the GUI’s previous/next different-region filters.

## Saccade direction

EyeSort finds the nearest preceding or following configured saccade within the trial and computes end-X minus start-X. Forward is positive for left-to-right and negative for right-to-left.

The 1.0 code uses hidden minimum amplitudes:

- incoming direction requires absolute movement greater than **5 px**;
- outgoing direction requires absolute movement greater than **10 px**.

A missing qualifying saccade causes a selected direction filter to fail. Leaving both direction boxes unchecked means any direction and does not require the threshold.

## Labels remain fixation-only

These criteria decide whether a fixation candidate is labeled; they do not assign codes to the saccade event. Report direction criteria and thresholds when they affect inclusion.
