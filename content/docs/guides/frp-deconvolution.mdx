## Preserve canonical fields

For models that accept string or event-level predictors, `EEG.event.type` may be descriptive or restored to the original marker. Keep `eyesort_full_code` unchanged as the stable EyeSort identity, and retain `original_type` for traceability.

Useful predictors include `current_region`, `region_pass_number`, `fixation_in_pass`, regression flags, previous/next region fields, condition/item/trial numbers, word text/index, and imported IA covariates.

## Model the scientific problem

EyeSort labels behavior; it does not deconvolve temporally overlapping brain responses. When event classes differ in fixation duration, saccade amplitude, timing, or participant/item prevalence, choose a model that handles those differences.

## Reporting checklist

Report:

- EyeSort version and upstream synchronization/event-detection method;
- exact region geometry and label criteria, including saccade thresholds when used;
- event-marker format and conflict policy;
- post-EyeSort event/epoch counts by condition and behavior;
- participant- and item-level distributions of target behaviors;
- shared text-IA and label-queue files;
- downstream overlap, artifact, epoching, and statistical methods.

Explain the behavioral-specificity versus power tradeoff. A narrow label can be theoretically precise while leaving too few or unbalanced observations for reliable inference.
